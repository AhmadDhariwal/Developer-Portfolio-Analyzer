### 2.3 Route Duplication & Compatibility Matrix Summary

- **Complete Duplication:** 9 endpoints under `/api/recruiter/*` overlap conceptually with `/api/recruiter-hub/*` (dashboard, candidates list/detail, jobs CRUD, matching).
- **Zero Active Frontend Consumers for `/api/recruiter/*`:** Verified by `git grep` across `frontend/src/`. Only `ApiService` still declares wrapper methods.
- **Compatibility Module (`backend/src/modules/recruiter/compatibility/`):**
  - Strictly re-exports `services/matching/aiRankingService.js` and `utils/matching/*` (`explanationEngine`, `featureExtractor`, `scoringEngine`, `weightConfig`).
  - Only referenced by `backend/src/tests/recruiter/aiCompatibilityPaths.test.js`.
- **Recommendation (Read-Only Assessment):** Do **not** remove `/api/recruiter/*` or `compatibility/*` until `adminConsoleService.js` telemetry (`aiRankCalls` / `matchCalls`) and any external API/test contracts are migrated to `/api/recruiter-hub/*`, and until ownership/scoping bugs in `/api/recruiter/*` are resolved or routes are formally deprecated.
