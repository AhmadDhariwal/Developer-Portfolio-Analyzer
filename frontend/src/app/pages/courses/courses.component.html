How recommendations and learning records work

Courses are ranked by the backend using career context, target skills and available evidence. Ranking scores describe ordering, not readiness or proficiency. Skill evidence may be incomplete or unavailable.

Source pools may be cached. “Loaded” shows when this page received a response, not when your skill evidence or a provider changed. Refresh requests a new source pool where provider availability permits. Curated courses remain available when an external provider cannot respond.

Provider reviews and derived video engagement are separate signals. Unknown metadata stays unknown. Curated availability is unverified; check the provider before enrolling.

Saved status, notes and optional percentages are your own records. Completion is self-reported and does not verify skills, create certificates or publish portfolio credentials. Save a course before adding it to Career Sprint.
