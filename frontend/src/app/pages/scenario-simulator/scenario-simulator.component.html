Simulator Mechanics & Planning Principles

01

What this sandbox does

Simulates hypothetical skill and project combinations using explicit baseline assumptions. All calculations are deterministic, explainable, and bound by realistic time and scope constraints.

02

How scoring works

  • Skills are weighted by role relevance, demand multiplier, and experience level.
  • Projects gain credit from technical complexity, timeline, and delivery impact.
  • Aligned skills and projects unlock synergy bonuses.
  • Overloaded scenarios trigger diminishing returns and heuristic penalties.
03

How prefills are used

Observations from Resume Analyzer, GitHub, Skill Gap, Recommendations, and Sprints provide helpful initial suggestions. They never overwrite your active scores or profile.

04

Best simulator habits

  • Focus on 2 to 4 high-relevance skills rather than a broad list.
  • Pair 1 flagship project directly with the skills you want employers to notice.
  • Check the sensitivity band and feasibility rating before committing your effort.
