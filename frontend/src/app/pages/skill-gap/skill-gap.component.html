Scoring methodology & provenance

Required skill coverage

Coverage = 100 × Σ(required weight × evidence credit) / Σ(required weight)

Demonstrated: 1 · Partial: 0.5 · Claimed only: 0 · Not evidenced: 0. Unavailable sources are disclosed separately. Optional skills are excluded from the denominator. Rounding and score boundaries are applied by the shared backend scoring utilities.

ATS scores, stars, repository count, and missing optional integrations do not directly affect coverage. Unknown or unavailable evidence is never presented as a proficiency score.
