- **Cycle Integrity (`0` Circular Dependencies):** All 20 CommonJS cycles identified in Phase 1 have been eliminated via leaf constants (`newsProviderKeys.js`, `courseCatalogConstants.js`), transactional primitive extraction (`notificationTransactionService.js`), and explicit registration hooks (`weeklyReportDeliveryService.js`, `newsDiscoveryService.js`, `scenarioSprintExportService.js`, `sharedCourseCatalogService.js`, `courseCatalogCoverageService.js`, `courseCatalogSearchService.js`).
